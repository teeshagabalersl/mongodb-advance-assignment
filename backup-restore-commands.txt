Task 6: Backup and Restore

6.1 Back up company_advanced to company_advanced.archive using gzip.
mongodump --db company_advanced --archive=company_advanced.archive --gzip

6.2 Restore it into a new database named company_advanced_restore.
mongorestore --archive=company_advanced.archive --gzip --nsFrom="company_advanced.*" --nsTo="company_advanced_restore.*"

6.3 Verify restored employees count.
mongosh --eval 'db.getSiblingDB("company_advanced_restore").employees.countDocuments()'

6.4 Verify restored departments count.
mongosh --eval 'db.getSiblingDB("company_advanced_restore").departments.countDocuments()'

6.5 Verify restored sessions count.
mongosh --eval 'db.getSiblingDB("company_advanced_restore").sessions.countDocuments()'
